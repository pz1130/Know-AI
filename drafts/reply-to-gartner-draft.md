Thanks for sharing this.

The framing matches how we have been thinking about it: flexible tools for requirement discovery, production-grade frameworks for orchestration, commercial solutions only where a well-defined complex domain justifies them. The caution on OpenClaw and Hermes is worth taking seriously — isolated environments, and control over what content goes in.

One line stands out: business is responsible for clarifying requirements **together with IT**. That "together" depends on whether we can triage a request ourselves. When we cannot, the conversation defaults to over-promising or handing the decision to a vendor.

That is what I have been working on. It is now online and open to all of us: **[SITE URL]**

## 中文

**主题：** Re: FW: Gartner 会议纪要

谢谢转发。

Gartner 的分层和我们的思路是一致的：需求探索用灵活工具，生产化交给编排框架，只有边界清晰的复杂场景才值得买商业方案。关于 OpenClaw、Hermes 的安全提醒值得当真——限制在隔离环境，并管住进入这些工具的内容。

里面有一句话我想拎出来：业务负责澄清需求，但要 **together with IT**。这个"together"成不成立，取决于我们自己能不能对需求做分诊。做不到的时候，对话通常只剩两个结局——要么过度承诺，要么把判断交给供应商。

这也是我最近在做的事，现在已经上线，大家都可以访问：**[SITE URL]**

它回答的是落地问题：接到一个需求，该做成什么形态、需要哪些组件、工期大概多少、什么时候应该劝退。另外有分诊决策树、业务同事真实说法的场景对照库，以及一份内训用的术语速查。
